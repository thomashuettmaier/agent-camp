Pixel Art Character Description

Overall impression: A young man, slim and tall, wearing modern Bavarian-Austrian traditional dress (Tracht). He stands facing the camera, relaxed, with a big smile. The style is "Tracht meets streetwear": a classic Janker jacket and lederhosen, paired with white sneakers and tinted sunglasses.

## Head & Face
Hair: Medium brown with a slightly warm tone. It's short on the sides, longer on top, and combed or gelled back with volume, so it looks neat and smooth with a slight sheen.
Face: Narrow, oval face with a light, slightly warm skin tone. The ears are visible and sit fairly close to the head.
Expression: A wide, open smile showing the upper teeth, with the cheeks slightly raised.
Glasses: Round panto-style sunglasses with tortoiseshell frames (havana brown with darker speckles). The lenses are a light blue-lilac tint, so the eyes show faintly through them.

## Upper Body (layers from inside out)
Shirt: White traditional shirt with a stand-up collar. The collar has a subtle light pattern, and a bit of white peeks out below the vest at the waist.
Vest: Grey-lilac traditional vest with a small, dense ornamental pattern (paisley or damask style) in beige, light grey and mauve. It has a small stand-up collar and olive-green piping (velvet trim) around the edges. The front is closed with a row of many small, round, olive-green buttons set close together.
Janker (jacket): Dark charcoal grey wool (loden), worn open, ending around hip height.
- Collar: The stand-up collar is dark forest green, edged with thin olive or gold piping.
- Buttons: Five round stag-horn buttons down the front, cream-white with a light brown rim.
- Details: A flap pocket at the hip with green piping, and a yoke seam suggested at the shoulder.

## Arms & Hands
His right arm (left side of the image) hangs loosely, with the hand tucked into the front pocket of the lederhosen so only the knuckles show.
His left arm is mostly hidden by the jacket and also hangs relaxed at his side.

## Lederhosen
Color & material: Light sand or tan suede with a clearly worn-in patina: blotchy lighter and darker patches, especially on the thighs.
Length: Short, ending just above the knee, with a slightly flared, curved hem.
Front flap (Hosentürl): A classic drop-front flap with cream decorative stitching and two horn buttons at the top of the waistband.
Embroidery: Dark green vine-like leaf embroidery (oak leaf or tendril motif) curving across the flap and sides. Light cream decorative stitching follows the outlines alongside it.
Side details: Decorative lacing at the outer leg hem, with two small horn buttons or little straps.
No suspenders are visible.

## Legs & Shoes
Legs: Bare and slim, the same light skin tone as the face.
Socks: Short white ankle socks, barely visible.
Shoes: White leather low-top sneakers with white soles and white laces. The heel tab and collar are navy blue, and there's a small, subtle logo text on the side.

## Pose
He stands upright and faces forward, with his weight on both legs, feet about shoulder-width apart and toes pointed slightly outward. His head tilts very slightly, and the whole stance looks relaxed and cheerful.
